Uma’s Block Planner

Send it to her calendar

Builds a file with every school day, practice, lesson and anything she has added — then imports in one go. Re-importing later updates the same events instead of duplicating them.

How to import

Google Calendar — Settings → Import & export → Import, pick the .csv, choose which calendar, Import.

Apple Calendar or Outlook — unzip the download, then open the .ics inside. On iPhone: tap the zip in Files, tap the .ics, then “Add All”.

Tip: import into a calendar of its own (call it “School”) so it can be toggled off or replaced in one step.

Where the fixed schedule comes from. Canyon High School 2026–27 block calendar and the district calendar — odd and even days, minimum days, breaks and no-school days are taken straight from them.

Practice rule. Lacrosse runs Monday, Wednesday and Thursday through the last day before winter break (Dec 18). Odd days: Upper Fields, 1:45–3:45. Even days: Crescent Elementary, 3:45–5:45. No practice on a no-school day.

Piano rule. Wednesdays at 1:45, one hour, on any school Wednesday when practice is not at 1:45 — which works out to even Wednesdays during the season, and every Wednesday once the season ends. Change the length or drop a week by adding your own entry over it.
